    ✨ Why marge-PDF is Free

    And always will be

    🆓
    No limits. Ever.

    Merge 5, 10, or 50 files — free, always. No daily quotas, no file-size limits, no sign-up, no email, no credit card.

    🔒
    Your files never leave your device

    Unlike iLovePDF, Smallpdf, and most other services, we don't upload your files to any server. Everything happens directly in your browser.

    💰
    So how does it survive?

    We have almost zero costs — no servers, no bandwidth bills, no databases. Free forever, supported only by optional donations and a small ad banner.
