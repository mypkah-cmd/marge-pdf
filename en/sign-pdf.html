How to sign a PDF — 3 simple steps

  1. Open the "Signature" tool — click the ✍️ icon on the home page.
  2. Create your signature — choose one of three ways: draw with mouse/finger, type your name in a script font, or upload a PNG image.
  3. Place it on the PDF — click on the page where the signature should go, then hit "Place on PDF".

That's it. No signup, no email confirmation, no "choose your plan".

Frequently asked questions

Is this signature legally valid?

For most everyday cases (contracts between individuals, internal documents) — yes. For strict legal cases (notarized deals, bank contracts) a digital certificate (QES/eIDAS) may be required — check with the organization.

Can I sign on multiple pages?

Yes. After loading the PDF, click on each page where you need a signature, then hit "Place on PDF".

Will the original PDF quality be preserved?

Yes. We add the signature as an image on top of the page. The rest of the text, images, and layout stay untouched.

What if I misplace the signature?

Just reload the PDF and place it again. The original file isn't modified until you hit "Place on PDF".

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. You can draw the signature with your finger right on the phone screen — perfect if you're signing a document on the go.
