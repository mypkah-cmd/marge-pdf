Frequently asked questions

What JPG quality do I get?

High. We render PDF at ×2 scale (about 144 DPI) and save as JPG with 92% quality. Enough for A4 printing and web publishing.

How do I download a multi-page PDF?

If the PDF has more than one page, each becomes a separate JPG, and all are automatically packed into a ZIP archive. Unzip it — you'll see files like `page-001.jpg`, `page-002.jpg`, and so on.

Can I convert only certain pages?

Currently all pages are converted. If you need select pages, first use the "Extract Pages" tool to get a PDF with just those pages, then convert.

Is the original PDF quality preserved?

Yes, we don't touch the source PDF — we only read it and create new JPG files. The original stays on your device unchanged.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Open in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android). For PDFs larger than ~20 pages, a computer is recommended.
