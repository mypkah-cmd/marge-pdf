How to add a watermark — 3 simple steps

  1. Open the "Watermark" tool — click the 💧 icon on the home page.
  2. Set the text and opacity — for example, "DRAFT" and opacity 0.3.
  3. Download the PDF — the mark is applied diagonally to every page automatically.

That's it. No signup, no email, no "choose your plan".

Why marge-PDF instead of iLovePDF or Adobe

Most online watermark services upload your PDF to their servers. When you're marking a confidential document, it matters that it doesn't end up on someone else's machine.

marge-PDF works differently:

  • Files stay on your device. No servers, no uploads. Everything runs in your browser.
  • Adjustable opacity. From 0.1 (barely visible, like copy protection) to 1 (solid stamp).
  • All pages at once. No need to click through each page — the mark goes on the whole document.
  • Free forever. No subscription, no file-count limits.
💡 Tip: a watermark protects against careless use, not against attackers. For serious protection use a password (the "Protect PDF" tool) along with the mark.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate or move the watermark?

By default, the mark sits diagonally at 45° — the classic format that's readable but doesn't obscure content. Position is fixed for now; customization is coming in a future update.

What color can I choose?

Currently grey (RGB 50%, 50%, 50%) — neutral and unobtrusive. For a colored stamp, use the "Stamp" tool, where green, red, blue and other colors are available.

Does the watermark affect file size?

Barely. Only text is added — a few kilobytes. If the source PDF was 2 MB, after adding the watermark it stays roughly the same.

Can I remove the watermark later?

The mark is embedded in the PDF as part of the page content. Removing it requires recreating the document — either in an editor or by re-converting from the original.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Open in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) — the watermark is applied the same as on desktop.
