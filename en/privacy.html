Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Who we are

marge-PDF (margepdf.com) is operated by KoT. Contact: margepdf@gmail.com.

Your files

PDF and image files you open in the tools are processed locally in your browser. They are not uploaded to our servers. Tool libraries are loaded from the cdnjs.cloudflare.com CDN, so that provider can see your IP address and the fact that you loaded the page.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 with IP anonymization to understand how the site is used. The Google Analytics script is loaded only after you click “Accept” in the cookie banner. If you decline, no analytics events are recorded. Your choice is stored in your browser; you can change it by clearing the site’s data.

Data stored on your device

Your browser keeps your language, theme, cookie choice, favorite tools and, if you enable it, a history of recent activity. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Donations

Donations are optional and handled by PayPal under PayPal’s own privacy policy. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Contacting us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, withdraw consent at any time, and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Write to margepdf@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
