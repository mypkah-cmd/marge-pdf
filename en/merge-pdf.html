Frequently asked questions

Is there a limit on the number of files?

No. Merge as many as you need.

What's the maximum size of a single PDF?

The limit is your device's RAM. Typically 500 MB–1 GB. Less on a weak phone.

What happens to my files after merging?

Nothing. They stay on your device — nothing is sent anywhere. After you close the tab, everything disappears from browser memory.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

If a PDF is password-protected, first remove the protection using the Protect PDF tool, then merge.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Open the site in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) — everything works the same.
